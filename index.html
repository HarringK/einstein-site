Albert Einstein

A theoretical physicist who was born in Germany and is best known for developing the theory of relativity. His mass-energy equivalence formula E=mc^2 is considered the worlds most famous equation. He won the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1921 for his contributions to theoretical physics.
